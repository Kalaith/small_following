# Just a Small Following - beat sheet (user direction, 2026-10-07)

Supplied by the user as the creative direction for the music video, kept
verbatim below. `song.json` holds the singable lyrics and the shot list
derived from it; `docs/MUSIC_VIDEO.md` section 3 explains how each gag is
staged in-engine, what stays real gameplay, and where an interpretation was
needed (for example the failed password entry).

---

## Music Video Beat Sheet

### Opening
**Lyric:**  
*Woke up in Bramblewick, hood upon my head...*

Open on an absurdly modest cult headquarters.

Not a temple. More like:
- one folding table
- one candle
- one handwritten sign
- one robe hanging from a coat hook
- a biscuit tin
- three mismatched chairs

The lead cultist wakes up at the table as though they have been sleeping there.

A little countdown appears:

**00:11**

They immediately panic and rush outside.

---

## Verse 1: Recruitment

### "I asked the garden club..."

The garden club is genuinely just three elderly people tending flowers.

The cultist gives them an enthusiastic pitch.

One shrugs.

One nods politely.

One is mostly interested in the free biscuit.

### "Three of them said maybe..."

Cut immediately to:

**FOLLOWERS: 3**

The cultist celebrates like they just won an election.

Confetti cannon.

It barely works.

One piece of confetti falls out.

### "They brought along nine coins..."

Someone empties nine coins into a ceremonial bowl.

Huge dramatic close-up.

The cultist counts them twice.

### "And Gerald brought a pie."

Gerald enters carrying a pie.

Nobody reacts.

He just puts it on the table.

The camera holds on the pie slightly too long.

---

# Pre-Chorus

### "Draw a little circle..."

The cultist draws a ritual circle.

It is noticeably wonky.

They rotate the rug underneath it instead of fixing the circle.

### "Talking One is written down..."

A glowing rune appears.

The cultist immediately starts talking much faster.

Everyone else slowly regrets this upgrade.

One follower quietly puts their biscuit down.

---

# Chorus 1

### "It's just a small following..."

Wide shot.

Still just four people.

Very reasonable.

The lead gestures proudly toward them.

### "Nothing alarming..."

Behind them, someone is trying to put out a tiny fire.

Nobody acknowledges it.

### "We've got biscuits and a robe."

Someone opens the biscuit tin.

There is exactly one biscuit left.

Two followers look at each other.

### "And in eleven seconds..."

Countdown returns.

**00:11**

The cultist instantly spins around and recruits someone walking past.

They get:

**+1 FOLLOWER**

A tiny celebratory trumpet sound.

---

# Verse 2: Expansion

### "Some merchants from the square..."

Now the cult has rented a stall.

The sign still says:

**SMALL FOLLOWING**

Except someone has squeezed:

**VERY**

above it in smaller handwriting.

There are maybe twelve people now.

### "I hired someone dressed in teal..."

Teal Cultist enters dramatically.

They clearly believe this is now their music video.

Wind machine.

Hair blowing.

Spotlight.

They step forward.

### Teal Cultist:
**"Join us! Join us!"**

Lead immediately pushes them gently back into formation.

### Lead:
**"That's enough."**

Teal cultist looks devastated.

---

# Pre-Chorus 2

### "Make the circle bigger..."

The original ritual circle no longer fits everyone.

Followers start extending it with chalk.

Then more chalk.

Then someone arrives with a bucket of paint.

### "Move the chairs aside..."

There are suddenly dozens of chairs.

The cultist keeps moving them off-camera.

More chairs keep appearing.

### "We're growing worldwide..."

Huge triumphant map reveal.

Three pins.

All within the same village.

Tiny subtitle:

**\*within walking distance**

One pin is literally across the street.

---

# Chorus 2

### "It's just a small following..."

Now there are perhaps thirty people.

The camera framing becomes suspiciously tight to hide them.

### "Nothing alarming..."

A huge banner unfurls behind the lead.

It reads:

**NOTHING ALARMING**

Two followers struggle to hold it straight.

### "Please ignore the extra robes."

Rack of robes rolls through the background.

Then another rack.

Then another.

The lead deliberately steps sideways to block them.

### "There's hardly anybody..."

Camera cuts wide.

Enormous crowd.

Immediate smash cut back to close-up.

### "We only moved the tables / So that everyone could fit."

Reveal that they have taken over the entire town square.

Tables everywhere.

Gerald still has the pie.

---

# Priest Bridge

Everything suddenly becomes absurdly serious.

Dark lighting.

Smoke.

Huge cathedral.

Priest standing dramatically above everyone.

### Priest:
**"No."**

The cult gasps.

### Cult:
**"OBJECTION!"**

A follower slams both hands on a tiny portable lectern.

### Priest:
**"Absolutely no."**

### Cult:
**"OBJECTION!"**

More dramatic.

Someone rings a bell.

### Priest:
**"I said no."**

### Cult:
**"OBJECTION!"**

Long silence.

The Priest looks at:
- the paperwork
- the crowd
- the cultist
- Gerald
- Gerald's pie

### Priest:
**"…fine."**

Tiny bell.

Everyone silently fist-pumps.

The priest immediately regrets everything.

---

# Chorus 3: Bellmarket

Key change.

Completely disproportionate production upgrade.

Pyrotechnics.

Choir.

Banners.

Feathers.

Marching followers.

### "It's just a small following!"

There are now hundreds.

The lead continues looking directly into camera with complete sincerity.

### "A perfectly small following!"

Followers march past carrying a gigantic ceremonial statue.

It takes twenty people.

### "Bellmarket is waiting..."

Mass procession heads toward Bellmarket.

Local residents watch them arrive with growing concern.

### "There's really not that many!"

Someone hands the lead a clipboard.

They look at the follower count.

Pause.

Slowly turn clipboard face-down.

### Whisper:
**"How many?"**

Lead:

**"…stop counting."**

Music continues immediately.

---

# Spoiler Breakdown

Everything freezes.

Music drops out.

Lead leans into camera.

### "Psst."

Camera zooms closer.

### "Don't feel like doing the village?"

They look around suspiciously.

### "Don't tell the dev."

They pull out a tiny scrap of paper.

**PASSWORD: PLZKTKS**

They enter it.

Huge magical seal.

Nothing happens.

Message:

**THAT PASSWORD DID NOT OPEN THE SEAL.**

Long silence.

### Lead:
**"…right."**

Checks paper.

Turns it upside down.

### Lead:
**"Not that one."**

---

# Password Chant

Suddenly:

FULL CHOIR.

Ridiculous production.

Dozens of cultists arranged on temple stairs.

Each letter gets its own dramatic cut.

**P!**

Lightning.

**L!**

Drum hit.

**Z!**

Bell.

**K!**

Fire.

**T!**

Teal cultist gets another solo shot.

**K!**

Lead pushes them out of frame.

**S!**

Huge explosion of confetti.

Then everyone joyfully chants:

**PLEASE!**

**OKAY!**

**THANKS!**

The seal opens.

Tiny subtitle:

**Developer convenience feature**

Nobody acknowledges it.

---

# "Someday" Verse

Complete tonal shift.

Warm dreamy lighting.

The lead imagines future locations.

### "Someday, a garden..."

Beautiful garden.

Followers having tea.

Gerald arrives.

Still carrying the same pie.

### "A harbour of captains..."

Ships in the distance.

Cultists waving from docks.

### "A fair full of friends..."

Market stalls.

Games.

People laughing.

One stall is clearly cult recruitment.

### "A tower of stargazers..."

Followers looking through telescopes.

One telescope is pointed directly at another telescope.

### "An abbey up high..."

Huge peaceful abbey.

Only four cultists visible.

For once, genuinely small again.

### "Some helpful little helpers..."

A dozen assistants emerge.

Then another dozen.

Lead watches them keep arriving.

### "And some magic... probably."

Someone attempts magic.

Produces one spark.

Everyone politely applauds.

---

# "Or twenty"

The dreamy vision becomes enormous.

Hundreds of followers.

Great towers.

Ships.

Markets.

Banners.

Choirs.

Then:

### Lead:
**"Or twenty."**

Everything disappears.

Back to original folding table.

### Lead:
**"Twenty would be nice."**

Gerald sits beside them.

Pie untouched.

---

# Final Chorus

Back in the humble headquarters.

Except subtle signs suggest it is no longer humble:

- stacks of paperwork
- dozens of robes
- enormous donation chest
- several maps
- ceremonial staff
- queue extending off-screen

### "It's just a small following..."

Camera stays close.

### "A funny little following..."

Someone squeezes behind the lead carrying a giant golden idol.

Lead ignores this.

### "A handful of believers..."

Camera begins slowly pulling backward.

There are dozens.

Then hundreds.

Then the entire square.

### "And some biscuits in a tin."

Cut to biscuit tin.

Now industrial-sized.

---

# Final Tag

Music becomes tiny and gentle.

### Lead:
**"It's just a small following..."**

Camera keeps pulling back.

The procession stretches through Bramblewick.

Across a bridge.

Over a hill.

More banners appear in the distance.

Music stops.

One distant voice:

### "…for now."

Final shot:

The original handwritten sign:

**SMALL FOLLOWING**

Someone has added another plank underneath:

**PLEASE FORM AN ORDERLY QUEUE**

Cut to black.
